🤖 Automatic first greeting

When ON: the first time a new person writes to you in a private chat, your greeting text is sent to them automatically — plain text, once. This works for EVERYONE who messages you privately — saved in Customers Data or not, saved in your phone contacts or not. People you already chatted with, and anyone already greeted, get nothing.

Your greeting text

This text belongs to you only; every admin keeps their own. It is sent automatically when the switch above is ON — there is nothing to select.
