📸 Weekly Story

7 photos or videos once · one story per day · nothing repeats

1. Add your 7 photos or videos 0 / 7

Choose photos, videos or a mix of both — whatever you want for each day. All 7 at once (or in several steps). Item 1 becomes Day 1, item 2 Day 2 … Each one is posted once on its own day. Use ◀ ▶ to change the order.

Videos: any format (MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WebM, 3GP, WMV …) up to 60 seconds and 200 MB each. Every video is converted automatically so it plays on all phones; vertical (9:16) looks best.

2. When and how

It is posted from that admin's own Telegram account.

✏️ Edit plan

Days — change a caption, or replace the photo / video of a day that is not posted yet

Stories already posted on Telegram are never changed or posted again. If the post time has already passed today, today's story is posted right away.
