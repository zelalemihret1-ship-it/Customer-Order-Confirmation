Workshop, finishing, stock & driver placesLoading…
These settings are only yours. Choose three places in your Telegram groups (a group, or one topic of a group). A job goes first to the Workshop; when the workshop presses 🛠 Finished workshop it moves to Finishing; there ✅ Finished posts the product in your selected Stock group, where you can mark it Sold Out or Restock. The optional 🚚 Driver place (a group + its Driver topic) receives a finished order — or a sold readymade product — when you press 🚚 Send to driver.
All production and material messages are sent from YOUR connected Telegram account — never by the bot. The selected group/topic is only the destination.
