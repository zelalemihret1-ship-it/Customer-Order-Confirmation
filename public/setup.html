2. Telegram bot delivery

AMBAR sends all order cards using BOT_TOKEN only. No Telegram Business connection, TG_API_ID, TG_API_HASH, or personal Telegram login is required. Customers must press Start in the AMBAR bot before it can receive private bot messages. Owner/admin personal-account connection is not required.
